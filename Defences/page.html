The traps

Built from the things you already carry. Used-up traps look broken.
Tin-can alarm line
cans on a string. Rattles when a zombie touches it and tells you where
Spike pit
dug with a shovel. Zombies fall in, take a few stakes and stay down for a few seconds
Nail board
nails through planks. Slows and cuts what walks over it, and can puncture a vehicle's tyre

Every trap wears out. Right-click it to see how worn it is, repair it or take it down for part of its materials.

Sandbox: traps hurting players (off by default), tyre punctures, damage, durability and how far an alarm line is heard.

04

The Battery Pack

Fill the pack with any mix of three kinds of battery.

Makeshift240 Wh12 torch batteries, wears fast
Car battery720 Whany one you find
Heavy deep-cycle1,400 Whlasts 3x as long, cuts top speed

Mix kinds freely. Each battery keeps its own charge and health. Fit and pull them from the Battery pack menu.

Insulated box

Halves the cold's range loss. At -10 °C the pack keeps 78%, not 55%.

Pack balancer

Evens the batteries out on charge and spares the weakest one half its wear.

Cell meter

Volts, charge, health and heat for every battery, flagging WEAK, LOW and DEAD.

Hot days, heavy tows and a lowered plough heat it up. Past 100 °C it eases off. At 130 °C it stops until it cools to 70 °C.

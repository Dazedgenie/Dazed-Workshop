Beacons and logs

Leave a message on the air, and listen to everyone else's.
Signal Logger
records everything heard on its frequency. Anyone who finds it can read it
Beacon
set a message and call sign on a wired ham. Send once or every few hours. Perfect for trade posts
Cipher cards
a Scrambler and Descrambler keep a message for the people meant to read it

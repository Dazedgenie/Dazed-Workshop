03

Running Water

Bring a whole house's taps back to life.

The water main

Stand it outside a house, pipe it to a water tank and pick the building in Dazed Core's Building Picker. Once the town water is off, every sink, bath, shower, toilet and washer inside runs from your tank.

Green: servedYellow: click to addBlue: click to removeRed: out of reach

Water pumps

Hand or electric. Stand one on bare ground and it pumps ground water into a tank.

Drilled well

About 15 L of clean water a minute, wired to Dazed Power.

Purifier

Cleans dirty water on its way in. Fast with a filter cartridge fitted.

05

Better Together

Plumbing plugs into the rest of the Dazed suite.

Dazed Power

Generators burn straight from a propane or petrol tank, steam engines stay topped up, and pumps, purifier, well and electric fuel pump run off your controller.

Dazed Climate

Pipes and tanks freeze in the cold, and insulation slows it. A tank frozen over 80% full can crack and leak until welded.

Dazed Dank

Pipe a hydro reservoir to a water tank to fill it and keep it topped up.

Butchery & Cooking

The smoker smokes Butchery's cuts and dries jerky strips.

Welding recipes

Tanks, pumps, pipes and machines, from Welding 1 up to 7 for the biggest tanks.

3 sandbox options

Water main reach and flow, and whether pipe work needs a wrench. Or one Core preset.

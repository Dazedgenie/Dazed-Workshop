01

Making Power

Seven ways to fill your batteries, sun or no sun.

Solar

Fixed arrays, trackers that follow the sun for up to 15% more, and a big 2x2 array. Snow, dust and wear cut output.

Wind

Windmills turn to face the wind, blades spinning and creaking.

Pedal

Sit on it and ride. It makes power for as long as you keep pedalling.

Water wheel

A steady 350 W beside a river or lake. Slower in ice, stronger in rain.

Steam engine

Burns fuel and drinks water for strong, steady power.

Generators

Propane or petrol. Set AUTO to start low and stop when full. Makeshift ones are pull-start.

Car alternator

A parked car with its engine running feeds up to 500 W into the system.

Read the weather

A windsock and a weather vane show how hard and which way it blows.

05

Weather & Hazards

Electricity bites back if you get careless.

Live wiring

Wiring a live system can burn your hand.

Switch it off first.

Lightning

Storms can trip the isolator or damage the controller.

A grounding rod takes it.

Hydrogen

A Makeshift bank charged hard in a closed room builds gas.

Open a window.

Fumes

A generator under a roof poisons the building.

Run it outside.

Knox County Almanac

Read the sky for a 30-day forecast. Know when the sun, wind, rain and storms are coming before you plan your power.

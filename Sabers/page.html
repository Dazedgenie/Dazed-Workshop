2

Using it

Right-click the hilt to ignite it, or bind a key in Options.

A lit saber hums, glows in its colour and lights the floor around you.

Huge damage, cuts through several zombies a swing and makes short work of doors.

Runs on a power cell: about two in-game hours a charge. Put in vanilla batteries to recharge it.

Every sound is original, synthesised for this mod. No film audio.

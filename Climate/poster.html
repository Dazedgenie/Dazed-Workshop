05

Ice

With Dazed Plumbing, below freezing, water stops moving.

Pipes

Freeze in about 3 hours at -5 °C and pass nothing until they thaw. Pipe Insulation Wrap slows it fourfold.

Water tanks

The bigger, the slower. Frozen over 80% full, one may crack and leak until you weld it.

Fuel

Petrol and propane never freeze and keep flowing all winter.

Pipe Insulation Wrap turns up in plumbing and hardware stock, or make it from ripped sheets and duct tape.

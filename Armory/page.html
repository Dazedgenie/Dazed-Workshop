BB guns

Quiet, harmless-ish and everywhere there are toys.
Crossman 92
CO2 powered
18-round magazine
Pump Master
spring pump action
6 BBs a shell
Maroo M16 AEG
battery, semi and full auto
60-round magazine

They sting more than they kill: hits stagger zombies, and a close headshot can drop one.

Quiet. Zombies hear them only a few tiles away. Good for rabbits and birds.

Change the CO2 cartridge or battery from the right-click menu.

They wear and jam with age. A BB Gun Repair Kit sorts them.

Reloading

Spent brass comes back. Load your own.
Keep the brass
real guns drop casings and hulls, more as Reloading grows
Press or hand kit
9mm, .45, .38, .357, .44 and 12 gauge on a press, or a Lea Loader anywhere
Duds happen
pull a bad load apart with an inertia puller

Rifles too: .22 LR, 7.62x39, .30-06, .30 Carbine, 7.62x54R and .50 BMG.

Primers and powder are rare finds, the real limit on home loads.

Learn it at Reloading 2, or from the Handloader's Digest.

Make 12 gauge flares and nail strips too.
